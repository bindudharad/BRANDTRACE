# BRANDTRACE — Hackathon Judge Guide

**Project:** BRANDTRACE — Evidence-Aware Brand Governance and Content Review Platform  
**Hackathon:** Inkloom AI CodeAxis Round 2  
**Tagline:** *Every finding has a source. Every decision has an owner.*  

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Problem Solved

BRANDTRACE solves a critical operational challenge for modern marketing, communications, and legal teams: **governing content generated across decentralized human teams, external creative agencies, and generative AI tools before it reaches the public.**

### The 4 Core Brand Governance Failures:
1. **Unchecked Superlatives & Prohibited Claims:** Marketing drafts frequently use unvetted absolutes ("best in market", "zero risk", "guaranteed results") creating immediate regulatory, legal, and consumer trust risks.
2. **Unsupported Factual Claims:** Metric, SLA, and territory assertions are published without linking back to verified institutional evidence.
3. **Silent Evidence Expiry:** A claim valid in 2024 (e.g., "99.9% uptime per benchmark survey") becomes a false or misleading statement when that benchmark expires or is superseded.
4. **Untraceable & Ungated Approvals:** Sign-offs often happen in unlogged chat channels, leaving zero auditable proof of who authorized a high-risk claim or why an exception was granted.

## 2. Core Review Workflow & State Machine

BRANDTRACE enforces a strict, deterministic-first, human-gated lifecycle:

```
[Draft Submission]
       │
       ▼
[Deterministic Rule Engine] ──(Pattern checks, required disclaimers, superlatives)
       │
       ▼
[Evidence Ledger Matcher]   ──(Links claims to Fact IDs; flags OUTDATED or CONTRADICTED)
       │
       ▼
[Optional Offline/AI Triage]──(Identifies promotional tone without altering raw text)
       │
       ▼
[Interactive Human Review]  ──(Reviewer can: Accept exact-span edit, dismiss with reason, escalate)
       │
       ▼
[Server-Side Approval Gate] ──(HTTP 409 blocks approval if ANY High/Critical finding is unresolved)
       │
       ▼
[Immutable Audit Ledger]    ──(Permanent event log recording actor, timestamp, action, rationale)
```

## 3. Finding Statuses & Evidence Semantics

Every finding generated by BRANDTRACE is tied directly to a source in the brand constitution or fact ledger:

| Evidence Status | Precise Meaning | Reviewer Action Required |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **`SUPPORTED`** | Verified directly by an active, unexpired fact in the approved ledger. | None; finding is informational and does not deduct from health score. |
| **`CONTRADICTED`** | Directly contradicted by an exclusive approved fact (e.g., claiming "Available globally" when fact F-2026-01 explicitly confirms exclusive territory). | High-risk blocker; must be edited or retracted before approval. |
| **`OUTDATED`** | The fact supporting this claim has passed its `valid_until` expiration date. | Re-substantiation required with current benchmark or updated SLA document. |
| **`NEEDS_REVIEW`** | A broad territorial or scope claim where available evidence is narrower (non-exclusive), requiring human judgment. | Reviewer must confirm or provide specific regional qualification. |
| **`UNSUPPORTED`** | No approved evidence exists in the institutional ledger for this assertion. | Reviewer must provide citation or remove the unverified claim. |

## 4. The 3–5 Minute Judge Walkthrough

You can evaluate the entire end-to-end platform locally in under 5 minutes without needing any external API keys.

### Prerequisites:
- Windows PowerShell with Python 3.10+ and Node.js 18+.

### Setup (Terminal 1 — Backend):
```powershell
cd C:\Projects\BRANDTRACE\BRANDTRACE
.\.venv\Scripts\Activate.ps1
pip install -r backend\requirements.txt
$env:DATABASE_URL = "sqlite:///./brandtrace.db"
$env:AI_PROVIDER = "offline"
uvicorn app.main:app --app-dir backend --reload --port 8000
```
*Health verification:* Open `http://127.0.0.1:8000/api/health` -> `{"status":"ok","service":"brandtrace","version":"2.0.0"}`

### Setup (Terminal 2 — Frontend):
```powershell
cd C:\Projects\BRANDTRACE\BRANDTRACE
npm run dev
```
Open **`http://localhost:3000`** in your browser.

### Step-by-Step Demonstration Steps:

1. **Cinematic Hero & 3D Evidence Prism:**
   - Observe the Three.js faceted icosahedron with orbiting evidence nodes color-coded by governance status (Teal: Verified, Red: Contradicted, Amber: Needs Review, Green: Approved).
   - Click **"Enter the Workspace"**.
2. **Explore Pre-Seeded Content Review:**
   - Select the pre-seeded review: *"Manual QA — Email Campaign 02"* (or submit fresh test copy).
   - Notice the **Health Score** gauge (e.g. 52/100) reflecting unresolved high-severity violations.
3. **Inspect Exact-Span Finding & Suggestion Neutrality:**
   - Click the finding for `"best choice"` (`R-VOICE-01`).
   - Notice the suggested replacement is the exact neutral word `"effective"`, avoiding duplicate words upon acceptance.
   - Click **"Accept Correction"** -> Draft automatically updates to `"effective choice"`, health score increases, and a new entry is written to **Version History**.
4. **Test Disposition Reason Integrity:**
   - Select the missing required term finding (`R-TERM-02`).
   - Click **"Apply Edit"** or **"Dismiss Finding"** and enter a justification.
   - Verify that the disposition rationale is stored strictly in the audit metadata and **never overwrites draft text**.
5. **Test the Server-Side Approval Gate (HTTP 409):**
   - Switch the top header role selector to **Brand Manager**.
   - Navigate to the **Approval Queue** while a High/Critical finding remains open.
   - Click **"Approve Draft"** -> The server actively blocks the request with a `409 Conflict` error dialog: *"Cannot approve: review has unresolved HIGH/CRITICAL findings."*
6. **Resolve & Successfully Approve:**
   - Resolve or dismiss the remaining critical issues with documented reasons.
   - Re-attempt approval -> Approval succeeds, status transitions to `APPROVED`, and the document is locked.
7. **Inspect the Immutable Audit Trail:**
   - Click the **Audit History** tab.
   - Inspect the chronologically ordered event ledger detailing actor role, exact timestamp, action taken, and before/after version diffs.
8. **View the Claim Impact Map:**
   - Navigate to **Governance Insights** -> **Impact Map**.
   - See how changing an upstream rule or letting a fact expire dynamically cascades to flag every affected marketing asset across the organization.

## 6. Known Boundaries & Limitations

In the interest of full engineering transparency:
- **Simulated Role Authentication:** Roles (`contributor`, `reviewer`, `brand_manager`, `admin`) are simulated via the `X-Demo-Role` HTTP header to allow frictionless judge testing without account registration barriers.
- **OCR Pipeline:** Optical Character Recognition requires external `tesseract` binary installed on system PATH. When absent, the system gracefully reports OCR as unavailable while continuing text and palette extraction.
- **Production Deployment:** The platform is configured and verified for local execution. A production setup would require PostgreSQL, Gunicorn, and an enterprise identity provider (SSO/SAML).
